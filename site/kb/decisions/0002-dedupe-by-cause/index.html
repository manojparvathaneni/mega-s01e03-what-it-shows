Decision: same cause, not same subject

  1. Deduplicate export rows by report ID. A new customer report is not an export duplicate.
  2. Read the report and resolve its tenant in the directory. Use the tenant’s locale and booking-form version, not the reporter’s browser locale.
  3. Use dayFirst and causeOf from src/lib/kb/causes.ts. For a known day-first tenant, booking-v3 form reports are hint; app/email and booking-v2 form reports are parser.
  4. Merge only after establishing the same cause. Subject equality is neither necessary nor sufficient. sameCause is a guard for these known causes, not a universal classifier for every product the support desk handles.
  5. Unknown tenant, unsupported locale, or unclear behavior: keep the report separate and request evidence. Do not merge on a matching subject or silently call it parser.
