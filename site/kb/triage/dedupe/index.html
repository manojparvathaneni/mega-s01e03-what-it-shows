Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.

The rule

A report belongs with an open ticket when evidence establishes the same cause (decision 0002, replacing the unchanged decision 0001):

  1. read the report and look up its tenant, locale, booking-form version, and channel;
  2. for the known date causes, evaluate causeOf and check sameCause;
  3. merge only when the cause is established as the same; otherwise keep reports separate.
Matching subjects can hide different causes; different subjects can describe the same cause. Intake tags and browser locale are not substitutes for the tenant directory.

When you are unsure

Keep the report separate and request the missing evidence. An unknown tenant or unsupported case must not be merged on subject alone. The date classifier covers the known parser and hint problems, not every product handled by the desk.
